PRIVATE & CORPORATE BANKING

Real-time 2FA trade authorizations & liquidity orders

Wealth managers receive investment and liquidity requests on WhatsApp. VALNATIR intercepts the order, prompts in-chat biometric 2FA, tokenizes balances, and generates an immutable non-repudiation hash directly inside the core banking system.

Result: Zero social engineering fraud · Complete DORA and MiFID II compliance.
INSURANCE & ACCIDENT ASSISTANCE

Instant FNOL claims submission & photographic appraisal

The policyholder transmits accident photos via chat. VALNATIR validates EXIF metadata, GPS coordinates, and RFC 3161 timestamps to prevent fraud, instantly dispatching coverage to the designated adjuster or repair shop.

Result: Appraisal cycle shortened from 6 days to 25 minutes · Customer CSAT +44%.
HEALTHCARE & HOSPITAL NETWORKS

Confidential clinical triage & lab delivery (GDPR Art. 9)

Doctor-patient interaction for pre-operative prep and lab delivery. Special category health data is stripped prior to AI processing and escalated to encrypted ephemeral rooms with zero device storage footprint.

Result: 100% compliance with protected medical data · Zero data leakage to public LLMs.
