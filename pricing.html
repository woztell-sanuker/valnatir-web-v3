FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know before deploying

What does «Start with a single workflow» mean?

You do not need to overhaul your entire telephony or swap out CRM infrastructure. We identify the specific transaction that your DPO or Compliance Committee currently blocks (e.g. payment authorization, citizen claims, or expert loss adjustment) and deploy governance specifically around that isolated flow in 48 hours.

Do my clients or citizens need to install any new app?

Zero friction. The end-user communicates through their native WhatsApp application with complete transparency. All cryptographic governance and compliance moderation occur at origin via the VALNATIR platform.

Why is VALNATIR more accessible than legacy DCGA suites?

Legacy DCGA providers sell heavyweight, static archival architectures designed strictly for US SEC fines. VALNATIR is built on a lightweight, modular conversation-state engine that coexists with existing telecommunication channels, dramatically reducing infrastructure overhead and operational cost.
