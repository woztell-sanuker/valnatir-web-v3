SUB-3MS PRE-INFERENCE GATEWAY

Pre-Model Anonymization: How It Operates

Raw text written by employees or customers never leaves your perimeter unredacted. VALNATIR dynamically substitutes sensitive entities with volatile synthetic tokens before triggering inference.

  • Deterministic PII Interception: National IDs, bank accounts, credit cards, telephone numbers, and protected health identifiers are isolated at the gateway.
  • Inference over Sanitized Context: Large language models reason, classify, and formulate responses solely using anonymized semantic context.
  • Secure Local Re-hydration: Upon receiving model output, VALNATIR safely re-injects real customer entities within your secure perimeter prior to final message delivery.
* Mandatory preceptive note: No PII exposed. Data anonymized prior to reaching the model. Encryption keys and custody remain under your organization's control.
