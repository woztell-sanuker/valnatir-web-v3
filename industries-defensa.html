Result: Zero hazard from handset inspection by aggressors · Untampered judicial evidence.
TACTICAL COMMAND & MULTI-AGENCY CRISIS

Dynamic Incident Room with Post-Debrief Crypto-Purge

Rapid provisioning during disasters, counter-terror ops, or SAR missions. Interconnects local police, rescue teams, and health services in a governed room. At debrief, session keys are shredded and master logs sealed into court custody.

Result: Immediate coordination without software installs · Zero operational leaks.
CRIME SCENE INVESTIGATION & FORENSIC FILING

In Situ Crime Scene Evidence Recording & Timestamping

Patrol officers record witness statements and crime scene photographs via guided in-chat prompts. The system computes instant SHA-256 hashes with qualified RFC 3161 timestamps, shielding evidence from courtroom tampering objections.

Result: Fully admissible forensic reports · 50% paperwork time reduction at station.
